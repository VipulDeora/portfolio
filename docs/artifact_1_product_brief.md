# Artifact 1 — Product Brief

## Project Name
Personal Website

---

# Objective

Build a personal website that positions Vipul as a systems-oriented AI Product Manager who designs decision systems powered by data, AI, behavioral intelligence, and operational workflows.

The website should not behave like:
- a generic PM portfolio
- a resume website
- a startup landing page
- a personal blog

The website should demonstrate:
- how Vipul thinks
- how he frames problems
- how he designs systems
- how he operationalizes AI
- how he converts ambiguity into measurable business outcomes

---

# Primary Goal

Convince smart hiring managers, founders, and product leaders that Vipul can:
- solve ambiguous business problems
- design scalable AI/data-driven systems
- operationalize intelligence into workflows
- build products with measurable impact
- think structurally rather than feature-by-feature

---

# Core Positioning

## Primary Positioning Statement

I build decision systems powered by data, AI, and behavioral intelligence.

---

## Supporting Positioning

My work focuses on turning signals into actions and measurable business outcomes across fintech, enterprise workflows, and AI-enabled products.

---

# Core Mental Model

The website should repeatedly reinforce the following mental model:

Signals -> Decisions -> Actions -> Outcomes -> Feedback

This becomes:
- product philosophy
- AI philosophy
- systems philosophy
- narrative backbone of the website

---

# Target Audience

## Primary Audience
- AI Product hiring managers
- Product leaders
- Founders
- Recruiters hiring for AI/Data/Product roles
- Technical executives

---

## Secondary Audience
- Product peers
- Builders
- Operators
- Engineers interested in systems thinking
- People interested in operational AI

---

# Desired Visitor Reaction

The visitor should progressively feel:

## Stage 1
“This is not a generic PM portfolio.”

## Stage 2
“This person thinks in systems.”

## Stage 3
“He has actually built complex AI/data-driven products.”

## Stage 4
“He has original frameworks and product philosophy.”

## Stage 5
“I want to talk to this person.”

---

# Website Philosophy

The website should:
- feel intellectually calm
- reward deeper reading
- communicate structured thinking
- focus on systems rather than features
- focus on decisions rather than dashboards
- demonstrate operational AI understanding

The website should NOT:
- feel flashy
- feel startup-generic
- rely on AI buzzwords
- behave like a design showcase
- overuse animations
- look like a SaaS landing page

---

# Narrative Identity

Vipul should not be positioned as:
- “AI enthusiast”
- “Product leader”
- “Passionate PM”

Those are labels.

The real identity is:

Builder of decision systems.

Everything on the website should orbit around this idea.

---

# Strategic Differentiation

Most PM portfolios showcase:
- features
- screenshots
- UI
- responsibilities

This website should instead showcase:
- systems
- behavioral models
- decision frameworks
- operational leverage
- AI orchestration
- measurable business outcomes
- product philosophy

This is the core differentiator.

---

# Website Pillars

The website should stand on four primary pillars.

---

## Pillar 1 — Systems

Purpose:
Show what has been built.

Visitor takeaway:
“He can design and operationalize complex systems.”

Examples:
- Portfolio Growth Engine
- Brand Cleaner
- Rewards Decision Engine
- Connect AI Copilot

---

## Pillar 2 — Thinking

Purpose:
Show how Vipul thinks.

Visitor takeaway:
“He has original frameworks and first-principles thinking.”

Examples:
- AI Is Not The Product. Decision Quality Is.
- Static Customer Segmentation Is Broken.
- Enterprise AI Will Be Operational.
- Decision Systems vs Dashboards.
- Primitives + Tenets.

---

## Pillar 3 — Experiments

Purpose:
Show exploration and frontier thinking.

Visitor takeaway:
“He actively explores emerging systems.”

Examples:
- Dark Factory
- AI Product Teams
- Behavioral Economy Simulator
- Enterprise Copilot Systems

---

## Pillar 4 — Philosophy

Purpose:
Humanize the thinking without becoming autobiographical.

Visitor takeaway:
“He has a strong product philosophy.”

Themes:
- Systems over features
- Decision speed matters
- AI should improve operational leverage
- Feedback loops compound
- Most products optimize symptoms instead of root causes

---

# Content Strategy Principles

All content should:
- be high signal
- avoid fluff
- avoid generic PM language
- be structured and scannable
- communicate measurable impact
- reward deeper reading

The website should feel like:
- a systems notebook
- an operational AI journal
- a research-backed product lab
- a calm intellectual operating system

---

# Visual Personality

The website should feel:
- calm
- intelligent
- warm
- structured
- editorial
- thoughtful
- systems-oriented

It should not feel:
- cyberpunk
- futuristic for the sake of it
- glossy startup AI
- hyper-corporate
- over-designed

---

# Success Criteria

The website is successful if a visitor can understand within 30 seconds:
- what Vipul builds
- how he thinks
- what systems he has designed
- what measurable outcomes he has delivered

The website is highly successful if the visitor thinks:

“This person understands AI operationally and thinks differently about product systems.”
